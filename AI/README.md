# AI

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. AI aims to create systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.

**Objective:**
- To understand the fundamentals and applications of AI.
- To explore machine learning, deep learning, and other AI techniques.
- To develop intelligent systems that can solve real-world problems.
